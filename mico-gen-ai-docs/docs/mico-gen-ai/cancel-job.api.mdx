Cancel a pending or processing job and all its associated tasks.

**Important Notes:**
- Only jobs with status `pending` or `processing` can be canceled
- Jobs that are already `completed`, `failed`, or `canceled` cannot be canceled
- When a job is canceled, all associated tasks that are not yet completed will also be marked as canceled
- Tasks that are currently being processed by a worker may still complete before the cancellation takes effect
