# Popup Image Refinement: Two Modes for Maximum Impact

We're excited to introduce our dual-mode popup image refinement system, designed to give you the flexibility to optimize your marketing campaigns for either maximum ROI or highest visual quality. Whether you're focused on conversion rates or brand excellence, we have the perfect solution for your needs.

## Two Distinct Approaches

Our platform now offers two specialized refinement modes, each optimized for different marketing objectives:

### 🎯 **Highest ROI Mode**
*Optimized for conversion and performance*

### 🎨 **Highest Quality Mode** 
*Optimized for visual excellence*

## Highest ROI Mode: Conversion-Focused Optimization

### How It Works

The Highest ROI Mode uses a sophisticated two-stage AI process designed specifically for Japanese marketing effectiveness:

1. **ChatGPT Analysis**: Our system sends your image to ChatGPT with a specialized prompt that analyzes your popup from a Japanese marketing strategy perspective
2. **Gemini Enhancement**: The AI-generated suggestions are then processed by Gemini to implement the optimization recommendations

### Specialized Prompt Engineering

Our ROI mode uses this carefully crafted prompt:

```
"Context: You are the top online marketing strategy expert in Japan. Your task is to re-design a high-ROI and high-CTA advertisement to generate qualified property inquiries and viewings. The output image is a online pop-up advertisement for Japanese {Industry} marketing, the target audience is {Target} group Japanese people, the color scheme is {MainColor}. Input: Image URL: {URL}. 

STRICT CONSTRAINTS: 
- NEVER suggest adding, changing, removing, or modifying ANY TEXT content (wording, font, size, copy length)
- Do NOT alter layout or composition
- Make sure that image layer is not obscured by other layers

Visual optimization scope: Focus ONLY on visual appearance refinements
You MAY adjust: colors (backgrounds, fonts, accents, buttons), filters (contrast/brightness/saturation/shadows/tones), shapes and gradients, background styling/textures
You MAY refine figures (e.g., change gender/age/identity or swap human ↔ animal/mascot)

Suggestions style: Keep instructions simple, brief, and workable in a sentence. Start each suggestion with a verb such as "Change" or "Replace". Select the top 4 suggestions most likely to improve ROI and CTA.

Deliver: Return a list of JSON format, the list is the top 4 most important visual design suggestions as a list of JSON format, and the suggestions separate by comma, the output: "image variations suggestion":["suggestion 1","suggestion 2","suggestion 3","suggestion 4"]"
```

### Key Benefits of ROI Mode

- **Conversion-Optimized**: Every suggestion is designed to improve click-through rates and conversions
- **Strategic Approach**: Uses marketing expertise to guide visual improvements

## Highest Quality Mode: Visual Excellence Optimization

### How It Works

The Highest Quality Mode provides direct, high-resolution image enhancement using advanced AI processing:

1. **Direct Gemini Processing**: Your image goes straight to Gemini with quality-focused instructions
2. **Expert Designer Standards**: Output meets the highest professional design standards

### Quality-Focused Prompt

Our quality mode uses this precision-engineered prompt:

```
"Using the provided image, re-design a high-resolution online pop-up advertisement image for Japanese {Industry} marketing, it must meet the highest standards of the expert designer, the target audience is {Target} age group Japanese people, the color scheme is {MainColor}, ultra-realistic, with sharp focus on all details PRESERVE all existing text content, DO NOT MODIFY, ADD, REMOVE, OR REPLACE ANY text content."
```

### Key Benefits of Quality Mode

- **Professional Standards**: Meets expert designer quality benchmarks
- **High Resolution**: Ultra-realistic output with sharp detail focus

1. **ChatGPT分析**: システムが画像をChatGPTに送信し、日本のマーケティング戦略の観点からポップアップを分析する専門プロンプトを使用
2. **Gemini強化**: AI生成された提案がGeminiによって処理され、最適化推奨事項が実装される

ROIモードは、この慎重に作成されたプロンプトを使用します：

```
"Context: You are the top online marketing strategy expert in Japan. Your task is to re-design a high-ROI and high-CTA advertisement to generate qualified property inquiries and viewings. The output image is a online pop-up advertisement for Japanese {industry} marketing, the target audience is {Target} group Japanese people, the color scheme is {main_color}. Input: Image URL: {URL}. 

STRICT CONSTRAINTS: 
- NEVER suggest adding, changing, removing, or modifying ANY TEXT content (wording, font, size, copy length)
- Do NOT alter layout or composition
- Make sure that image layer is not obscured by other layers

Visual optimization scope: Focus ONLY on visual appearance refinements
You MAY adjust: colors (backgrounds, fonts, accents, buttons), filters (contrast/brightness/saturation/shadows/tones), shapes and gradients, background styling/textures
You MAY refine figures (e.g., change gender/age/identity or swap human ↔ animal/mascot)

Deliver: Return a list of JSON format, the list is the top 4 most important visual design suggestions as a list of JSON format, and the suggestions separate by comma, the output: "image variations suggestion":["suggestion 1","suggestion 2","suggestion 3","suggestion 4"]"
```

### ROIモードの主な利点

- **コンバージョン最適化**: すべての提案がクリックスルー率とコンバージョンの改善を目的としている
- **日本市場フォーカス**: 日本の消費者行動と好みに特化
- **テキスト保持**: 視覚要素を最適化しながら既存のコピーを維持
