# Questionnaire Image Testing Results: Gemini 2.5 Flash Image-"Nano Banana" Model Limitations

Our latest testing reveals significant challenges with the "nano banana" model when generating questionnaires from sketch templates. While the model shows promise in other areas, it struggles with the specific requirements of questionnaire generation from hand-drawn sketches.

<!--truncate-->

## Testing Overview

We conducted comprehensive testing of the "nano banana" model across various questionnaire sketch templates to evaluate its ability to generate clean, readable questionnaire images based on customer brand. The results highlight specific areas where the model falls short of expectations.

## Key Findings

### ❌ **Critical Limitations Identified**

- **Layout preservation issues**: Generated questionnaires lose the original structured layout, with title areas, image placeholders, and text fields becoming disorganized
- **Inconsistent element placement**: Form elements like option areas (checkboxes, radio buttons) are frequently misaligned or positioned incorrectly
- **Quality degradation**: Output images often appear blurry, distorted, or lose the crisp professional appearance needed for actual questionnaire use
- **Structural breakdown**: The model fails to maintain the logical flow between title areas, content sections, and interactive elements
